ECG Fundamentals Training Module

Slide 1: Introduction to ECG
Electrocardiography (ECG) is a non-invasive diagnostic tool that records the electrical activity of the heart. It provides valuable information about heart rhythm, rate, and electrical conduction.

Learning Objectives:
• Understand basic ECG principles
• Identify normal ECG components
• Apply electrode placement techniques

Slide 2: Heart Anatomy
The heart has four chambers: two atria and two ventricles. The electrical conduction system includes:
- SA node (sinoatrial node)
- AV node (atrioventricular node) 
- Bundle of His
- Purkinje fibers

Slide 3: ECG Components
The normal ECG consists of:
- P wave: Atrial depolarization
- QRS complex: Ventricular depolarization
- T wave: Ventricular repolarization
- PR interval: Time from P to QRS
- QT interval: Time from QRS to T

Assessment Questions:
1. What does the P wave represent?
2. What is the normal heart rate range for adults?
3. Which lead is placed on the right arm?
4. What is the normal PR interval duration?
