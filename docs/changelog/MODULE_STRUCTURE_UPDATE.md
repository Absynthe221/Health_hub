## 🎯 Module 8: ECG Case Studies (Intermediate) - Details

### **Overview:**
Comprehensive clinical case studies integrating ECG interpretation with patient presentations. Students apply their knowledge to real-world scenarios.

### **7 Slides:**

1. **Case 1: Chest Pain (STEMI)**
   - Content: Text + ECG
   - Duration: 10 minutes
   - Clinical scenario: 58yo male with crushing chest pain
   - ECG findings: Anterior STEMI with reciprocal changes
   - Management: Activate cath lab, dual antiplatelet therapy
   - Interactive: Step-by-step analysis with quiz

2. **Case 2: Palpitations (Atrial Fibrillation)**
   - Content: Text + ECG
   - Duration: 10 minutes
   - Clinical scenario: 72yo female with irregular palpitations
   - ECG findings: AF with rapid ventricular response
   - Management: Rate control, anticoagulation (CHA2DS2-VASc)
   - Interactive: Risk stratification quiz

3. **Case 3: Syncope (Complete Heart Block)**
   - Content: Text + ECG
   - Duration: 10 minutes
   - Clinical scenario: 65yo male with loss of consciousness
   - ECG findings: Complete AV dissociation, ventricular escape
   - Management: Permanent pacemaker implantation
   - Interactive: Diagnosis quiz

4. **Case 4: Shortness of Breath (Heart Failure)**
   - Content: Text + ECG
   - Duration: 10 minutes
   - Clinical scenario: 76yo female with progressive SOB
   - ECG findings: LBBB, poor R wave progression
   - Management: Diuretics, optimize HF meds, consider CRT-D
   - Interactive: Device therapy quiz

5. **Case 5: Drug Toxicity (Digoxin)**
   - Content: Text + ECG
   - Duration: 10 minutes
   - Clinical scenario: 82yo male with nausea, confusion
   - ECG findings: 'Reverse tick' ST depression, bigeminy
   - Management: Stop digoxin, Fab fragments if severe
   - Interactive: ECG finding identification

6. **Interactive Case Solving**
   - Content: Interactive
   - Duration: 15 minutes
   - 3 complex cases with progressive hints
   - Cases: Posterior STEMI, ARVC, AF in WPW
   - Scoring system: Points based on decisions
   - Real-time feedback

7. **Comprehensive Case Assessment**
   - Content: Interactive Assessment
   - Duration: 30 minutes
   - 10 comprehensive clinical cases
   - Time limit: 30 minutes
   - Passing score: 70%
   - Topics: STEMI, arrhythmias, blocks, toxicity, syndromes
